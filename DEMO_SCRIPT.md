# BuildPilot — 3-Minute Hackathon Demo Script
### AI-Powered Project Intelligence • Build Better. Defend Smarter.

This script provides a structured, second-by-second guide for demonstrating BuildPilot during the live hackathon judging session.

---

## Demo Timing & Stage Flow

```
+-----------------------------------------------------------------------------------------+
| [0:00 - 0:20]    Problem & Hook                                                         |
| [0:20 - 0:40]    Select Quick Demo Profile (e.g., Fullstack AI Student)                |
| [0:40 - 1:10]    Generate 3 AI Recommendations & Review Comparison Cards                |
| [1:10 - 1:40]    Open BuildPilot Project Workspace (Architecture + Tech Stack)          |
| [1:40 - 2:00]    Explore Sprint Roadmap & Interactive Checklist                         |
| [2:00 - 2:30]    Engage BuildPilot AI Mentor (Quick Prompts & Custom Questions)         |
| [2:30 - 2:50]    Review Viva Defense Preparation & Examiner Questions                   |
| [2:50 - 3:00]    Export Markdown Blueprint + Closing Value Statement                    |
+-----------------------------------------------------------------------------------------+
```

---

### Step 1: Problem & Hook (0:00 – 0:20)
* **Screen:** Landing page hero (`http://localhost:3000`).
* **Speaker Action:** Scroll slightly to show the modern glassmorphic interface and value proposition.
* **Speaker Script:**
  > "Judges, every final-year engineering student faces the same critical hurdle: choosing a capstone project that is unique, feasible within college deadlines, and defendable in front of viva examiners. Most students waste weeks picking generic ideas or impossible scopes. We built **BuildPilot** to give every student an instant, tailored, end-to-end engineering blueprint."

---

### Step 2: Select Quick Demo Profile (0:20 – 0:40)
* **Screen:** Scroll down to the Profile Form.
* **Speaker Action:** Click the **'⚡ Fullstack AI Student'** Quick Demo button (or IoT / Cybersecurity). Point out how skills (React, Next.js, Python, FastAPI, PostgreSQL), budget (Under ₹5,000), team size (2 members), and duration (3–4 months) populate automatically.
* **Speaker Script:**
  > "Rather than typing everything manually during this demo, I'll click our **Fullstack AI Student** preset. Notice how it sets exact constraints: 2 team members, 3–4 months timeline, and an open-source ₹0–₹5,000 budget."

---

### Step 3: Generate AI Recommendations (0:40 – 1:10)
* **Screen:** Click **'Generate 3 Project Blueprints'**.
* **Speaker Action:** Highlight the progressive animated loading states ("Analyzing skill profile", "Synthesizing architectures", "Validating constraints"). When the results load, show the 3 cards and the comparison breakdown.
* **Speaker Script:**
  > "Our backend securely queries OpenRouter with Google Gemini 2.5 Flash, strictly validating constraints. Within seconds, we have **exactly 3 defendable project blueprints**, scored across Skill Match, Feasibility, and Innovation. Let's look at Option 01—it leverages the student's exact FastAPI and React stack."

---

### Step 4: Open Project Workspace (1:10 – 1:40)
* **Screen:** Click **'Open BuildPilot Workspace'** on Option 01.
* **Speaker Action:** Scroll down to the Workspace. Point out the Project Overview, Problem & Solution cards, Tech Stack badges, and the **5-Tier System Architecture Diagram** (Client Tier, API Gateway, Application Logic, Data & AI, Deployment).
* **Speaker Script:**
  > "When the student selects a project, they unlock their dedicated **BuildPilot Project Workspace**. Instead of just a text description, BuildPilot provides a complete 5-tier architectural breakdown, categorized tech stack, and clear data flow."

---

### Step 5: Roadmap & Interactive Checklist (1:40 – 2:00)
* **Screen:** Scroll through the **Development Roadmap** and click into the **Implementation Checklist**.
* **Speaker Action:** Check off 1-2 tasks. Click **'Mark All Complete'** and then uncheck one. Point to the live progress bar calculation.
* **Speaker Script:**
  > "Here is our phased sprint roadmap from initial schema design to final deployment. The interactive checklist allows the student team to track progress task-by-task with live completion metrics."

---

### Step 6: Engage AI Mentor (2:00 – 2:30)
* **Screen:** Scroll to the **BuildPilot AI Mentor** console within the Workspace.
* **Speaker Action:** Click the quick action pill **'Design Database'** or **'Explain Architecture'**. Show the rapid response with structured key points, next steps, copyable code, and warnings. Click the **Copy Code** button.
* **Speaker Script:**
  > "Need help during implementation? The student has a built-in **AI Technical Mentor**. Because it inherits the active project blueprint, when I click 'Design Database', it doesn't give generic SQL—it generates the exact schema required for this project, complete with copyable code snippets and architectural trade-offs."

---

### Step 7: Viva Defense Prep (2:30 – 2:50)
* **Screen:** Scroll to the **Viva Defense Preparation** accordion cards.
* **Speaker Action:** Expand a question to show the examiner question, technical rationale, and sample defense answer.
* **Speaker Script:**
  > "The most intimidating part for students is the external viva examination. BuildPilot generates targeted examiner questions and strategic defense answers, training students to defend architectural choices and security trade-offs confidently."

---

### Step 8: Export Blueprint & Wrap Up (2:50 – 3:00)
* **Screen:** Scroll to the top/bottom workspace export buttons.
* **Speaker Action:** Click **'Export Blueprint (.md)'** (triggering download) or **'Export Chat (.json)'**.
* **Speaker Script:**
  > "Finally, students can export the complete project blueprint as a formatted Markdown report ready for academic submission. BuildPilot turns final-year chaos into structured engineering success. Thank you, and we welcome your questions!"
